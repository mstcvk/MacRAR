# Privacy Policy — Easy Mac Archiver / MacRAR

_Last updated: 10 October 2026_

Easy Mac Archiver (Mac App Store) and MacRAR (GitHub) are archive utilities developed by Mesut Çevik.

**No data collection.** The apps do not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.

**Your files stay on your Mac.** Archives you open, extract or create are processed locally by the bundled 7-Zip engine. Passwords you type for encrypted archives are kept in memory only while the archive window is open and are never written to disk or sent anywhere. Decrypted copies that Open, Quick Look and drag-out need are written to a temporary folder and removed when MacRAR quits normally.

**Folder permissions (Mac App Store edition).** Because the app is sandboxed, macOS asks you to grant access to folders outside Downloads. The app stores these permissions as security-scoped bookmarks on your Mac so it does not have to ask again. There is no in-app option to remove these permissions yet.

**Network.** The Mac App Store edition makes no network connections. The GitHub edition checks `api.github.com` once a day for a newer release (can be turned off in Settings); this request contains no personal data.

**Contact.** Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/mstcvk/MacRAR/issues or e-mail the developer via the App Store support link.
